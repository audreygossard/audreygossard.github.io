I am a PhD student in applied mathematics at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques Jean Leray, Nantes Université and Centre Inria de l'université de Rennes. My research focuses on asymptotic analysis and numerical methods for biomedical applications, with a particular interest in bioimpedance modelling and electroporation.
I am working under the supervision of Annabelle Collin and Clair Poignard.
